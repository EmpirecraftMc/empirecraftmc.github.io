Banned Mods

A list of banned modifications for Empirecraft. This list includes, but is not limited to, these mods:

  • Any Hack / Cheat Client
  • X ray or ESP
  • Fly / Speed Modification
  • Crystal Optimisers
  • Fast Xp
  • Free Cam
  • Replay Mod (Can be used for recording purposes, but no gameplay advantages)
  • Health Indicators
  • Damage Indicators
  • Auto Tool
  • Chestplate Swapper
  • Auto Totem
  • Minimaps
  • Anchor Optimisers
  • Auto Clickers
  • Most Types of Macros (Ask on discord for more info)
  • Ping Spoofing
